## Mactician 1.3.0 — Performance update

- Reduce repeated graphics-driver work in Global TFT with a Vulkan buffer-view
  cache, preserving graphics quality and your existing settings.
- Enable the optimization for Global TFT 18.1-5423749 and 18.3-5530794.
  Other editions and game builds keep the existing rendering path.
- Restore temporary Android graphics settings after the game closes, including
  recovery on the next launch after an interrupted session.
- Include all launcher fixes and performance-diagnostics improvements from 1.2.5.

Local M1 Max tests on TFT 18.1 measured 41.34 → 59.48 FPS in one planning
scene, with improvements in two early Trial fights. These are limited scene
measurements, not a promised gain for TFT 18.3 or every Mac and match.
